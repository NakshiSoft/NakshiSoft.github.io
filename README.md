## 🛠 Our Focus

At NakshiSoft, we operate at the intersection of management and creativity, specializing in two core domains:

### 💼 Business Solutions (ERP)
We design and implement robust, scalable, and highly customizable ERP systems. Our development philosophy is centered on:
* **Workflow Automation:** Streamlining complex business processes into efficient digital journeys.
* **Code Maintainability:** We prioritize clean, readable, and multi-line logic over condensed hacks to ensure the system is easy to modify and scale.
* **Deep Customization:** Tailoring core frameworks to meet the specific industrial needs of our clients.

### 🎮 Game Development
Beyond management software, we build immersive digital experiences. Our creative division focuses on:
* Engaging gameplay mechanics.
* Cross-platform performance.
* Innovative storytelling through interactive media.

## 🚀 Deployment & Local Setup

This site is hosted via **GitHub Pages**. To run the project locally for development:

1. **Clone the repository:**
   `git clone https://github.com/NakshiSoft/NakshiSoft.github.io.git`

2. **Navigate to the folder:**
   `cd NakshiSoft.github.io`

3. **Open the project:**
   Simply open `index.html` in your preferred web browser.
